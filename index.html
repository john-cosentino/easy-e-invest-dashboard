Decision discipline

How an idea earns its way forward

An interesting event is never treated as a trade by itself.

  1. 01TriggerFind a development worth investigating
  2. 02ConfirmVerify material facts independently
  3. 03VariablePredict what changes before choosing a ticker
  4. 04Stress testModifiers, experts, and contradictions
  5. 05Market checkLook for confirmation without chasing
  6. 06Entry windowWAIT · ACCEPTABLE · PREFERRED
  7. 07Paper testFreeze the prediction and score the outcome
WAIT

Thesis may be valid, but confirmation, price, or reward-to-invalidation is not good enough.

ACCEPTABLE

Evidence holds, price is not excessively extended, and a staged paper entry is defensible.

PREFERRED

Strong evidence plus a favorable retracement, support area, or consolidation.
